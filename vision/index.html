Our vision / physical × digital

Big possibilities.
One piece at a time.

We believe creative worlds can begin with something you can hold. A piece. A connection. A new way to play.

The foundation

A familiar form.
A new starting point.

The geometry of IKOS gives this idea a physical foundation: modular pieces that come together in three dimensions. Park City Salad explores how that building experience could grow through art, collecting, and digital storytelling.

The globe shown is a physical construction concept. Future digital features are planned, rather than available today.

What guides us

Hands first.
Imagination everywhere.

01 /

Make play tangible.

Start with real construction. Connecting pieces and discovering forms puts curiosity, spatial thinking, and creative problem-solving into your hands.

02 /

Give each creation a personality.

Explore distinctive artwork, characters, and themed series that invite people to collect, combine, and make something their own.

03 /

Let the story keep growing.

Imagine a companion digital experience that extends physical play into characters, stories, and shareable creations. This is the direction we’re exploring.

Curious about the concept?

What is Park City Salad?

A physical-first educational construction-toy platform in development in Park City, Utah, inspired by the geometry of IKOS.

Is the digital experience available?

The companion digital experience is a future concept. The vision is to connect physical creations with new forms of creative expression.
